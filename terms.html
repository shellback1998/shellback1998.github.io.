Purpose and access

Turing Observatory is a personal infrastructure-monitoring and development-lab application operated by Scott Gaudet. This public website provides information only; it does not grant access to the private dashboard or infrastructure.

Authorized operation

The dashboard is intended for the operator and anyone expressly authorized by him. Operational controls must be used only on infrastructure the operator is authorized to manage. Email integration is optional and requires the Google account holder’s consent.

Monitoring limitations

Monitoring depends on network connectivity, available infrastructure, and external services. Data can be delayed or unavailable, and notifications are not guaranteed to arrive. This personal application is not an emergency or safety-critical alerting service. Deployment and removal controls may change lab workloads and should be used after reviewing their scope and confirmations.
